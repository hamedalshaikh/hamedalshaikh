02 / WHAT I DO

From first thought
to finished experience.

A few areas that shape the way I approach digital work. These describe my interests and focus, not a claim of specific certifications or project results.

Web development

Building responsive web experiences with a focus on structure, performance, and maintainable code.

Interface design

Thinking through layouts, visual hierarchy, and interactions so products feel intuitive and considered.

Creative interactions

Using motion and micro-interactions to add character without getting in the way of usability.

Idea development

Breaking down an idea, identifying what matters, and shaping it into a practical digital direction.

Growing Techpromts and creating a clear home for its work, identity, and digital presence.
